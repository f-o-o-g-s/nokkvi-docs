The MilkDrop mode plays **MilkDrop presets**: the swirling, warping, feedback-driven visuals that Ryan Geiss's MilkDrop made famous in Winamp, and that a community has been writing presets for ever since. Nokkvi doesn't contain MilkDrop itself; it runs the presets through [OjoDrop](https://github.com/sho-run/ojodrop), a MilkDrop engine written in Rust, fed by Nokkvi's own audio.

## Turning it on

Press `V` (or click the visualizer button in the player bar) until the drop icon shows. The cycle is Off → Bars → Lines → Scope → MilkDrop → Off.

MilkDrop takes the cover art's place:

- On the **Queue** (and the **Radio** view), the preset replaces the now-playing cover. The cover shows while the first preset loads.
- In **[Theater Mode](/guides/theater-mode/)** it fills the panel: the whole window with the Cover Art setting on **Hide in Theater Mode**, or the square or stretched panel otherwise.

It only animates while music is playing and the panel is on screen. **Pause** freezes the picture; **Stop** gives the panel back to the cover. On other views, or with the window closed to the tray, nothing runs and no presets load.

With [lyrics](/guides/lyrics/) on, the text sits over the preset with the usual dimming layer above it. The frosted-cover backdrop is skipped, since there's no cover to frost.

## Changing presets

Presets change on their own:

- every **30 seconds** (the Preset Interval setting; `0` turns the timer off), and
- on each **new track** (the Change Preset With Track setting).

The old preset dissolves into the new one over **2 seconds** (the Preset Crossfade setting; `0` cuts straight over), with a different pattern each time: all at once, a sweep, a growing or closing ring, or blotches. Each preset's name pops up as a notification when it appears (turn that off with Show Preset Names). Presets are drawn shuffle-style: every preset once before any repeats, and never the same one twice in a row.

| Key | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `N` | Next Preset: jump to another preset |
| `P` | Previous Preset: go back through the presets you've seen |
| `Shift + M` | Lock Preset: keep this one until you press it again (the timer and track changes leave it alone) |

All three are [rebindable](/reference/hotkeys/) and do nothing outside MilkDrop mode. They also work in Theater Mode. From a script, `nokkvi preset next` (and `previous`, `lock`, `unlock`, `favorite`, `unfavorite`, `hide`) does the same; see the [CLI guide](/guides/cli/#misc).

## Curating

Right-click the preset (on the Queue cover, or in Theater Mode) for:

- **Next Preset**, **Previous Preset**, **Lock Preset** / **Unlock Preset**
- **Favorite Preset** / **Unfavorite Preset**: with the Presets setting on **favorites_only**, only your favorites are drawn (and all presets while you have none).
- **Never Show This Preset**: hides the preset on screen for good and moves on at once (if you're paused, the next one loads when you press play). Hide the last one and the cover comes back.

Next and Previous only work while music plays with MilkDrop on screen; otherwise a notification says so.

Hidden and favorite presets are saved in `~/.config/nokkvi/milkdrop/curation.toml`, a plain list you can read or edit (if you break its syntax, Nokkvi warns you and won't save over it until you fix it and restart):

```toml
hidden = ["Some Author - a preset that didn't work"]
favorites = ["Another Author - one I love"]
```

## nokkvi's own presets

Nineteen presets are made for Nokkvi and draw in **your theme's colours**: its background, accents and visualizer gradient. Change theme and the preset on screen recolours on the spot. Eight of them also use the **playing album's cover** (or the station's logo on the radio), and it follows each track change:

| `nokkvi - cover halo` | The cover as a glossy card leaning in a dark room, wobbling on kicks, with a sheen, a drop shadow and a frame that pulses with the bass; rays of its colours stream out behind it, lengthening with the music, with dust twinkling in them. |
| `nokkvi - cover kaleido` | An endless dive into a kaleidoscope shown as stained glass, lit by a lamp wandering behind it, with dark lead along the edges; the cover seeds the pattern, which keeps growing finer detail, and the fold count steps through 6, 8, 5 and 4 every twelve kicks. |
| `nokkvi - cover ripple` | The cover embossed under a lit pool's surface; each kick drops a big ripple across it, rain keeps falling in between (more with the treble), and the glints travel as the light wanders. |
| `nokkvi - cover deep dive` | A flight into the cover, planted at the vanishing point, as it grows into a lava field (dark crust over glowing cracks) that keeps sprouting finer detail; each kick stamps the waveform as a ring of heat that dives outward. |

| `nokkvi - chladni` | Sand on a vibrating metal plate, drifting into the plate's still lines to draw its figure; the music picks the figure and each kick retunes the plate, so the sand migrates to the next pattern. No cover. |
| `nokkvi - julia lace` | An endless glide down into a Julia set drawn as glowing lace on a ground plane, spiralling around a point it never reaches; loudness sets the pace and kicks brighten the glow. No cover. |

| `nokkvi - black holes`, `nokkvi - cover black holes` | A remake of Flexi's "black holes [sucking fractals for lunch] 2": four black holes bounce and collide, hopping on kicks, and the picture spirals into each of them. What they swallow is clouds in the theme's gradient, or the cover. |

They're built as light on a dark canvas (glowing trails, bright bands), so in light mode they keep your theme's **dark** colours: the panel stays a dark window in your theme's palette rather than washing out.

To see only these, set **Presets** to `nokkvi` (Settings → Visualizer → MilkDrop, or `preset_source = "nokkvi"` in `[visualizer.milkdrop]`).

Your own presets can use the same trick: name a colour `NOKKVI_BG`, `NOKKVI_SURFACE`, `NOKKVI_TEXT`, `NOKKVI_ACCENT`, `NOKKVI_HIGHLIGHT`, `NOKKVI_WARM` or `NOKKVI_RAMP0` to `NOKKVI_RAMP5` in shader code (it becomes a `vec3`), or add `_R`, `_G`, `_B` for a number in the equations. Sample the cover as `sampler_cover`. Name the file `nokkvi - something.json` and it joins the `nokkvi` set.

## The presets

Besides its own nineteen, Nokkvi ships about 450 presets built in: the pre-converted pack from [butterchurn-presets](https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn-presets) (the preset collection behind the Butterchurn web player), minus the ones known not to run and the ones that need extra images. The file names keep each preset's author credit, which is the name you see when it appears.

The presets are the work of the MilkDrop community; each author holds the copyright in their own preset. They have been shared freely for two decades, and Nokkvi follows the stance of projectM's preset collections: they're treated as freely redistributable, and any author who'd rather not have a preset included can [open an issue](https://github.com/f-o-o-g-s/nokkvi/issues) to have it removed.

### Adding your own

Drop Butterchurn-format `.json` presets into `~/.config/nokkvi/milkdrop/`. They join the rotation at the next login, or right away when you press the refresh key (`R`) in MilkDrop mode. A file with the same name as a built-in preset replaces it. A preset that fails to load is skipped for the rest of the session (the reason is in `~/.local/state/nokkvi/nokkvi.log`).

## Settings

Settings → Visualizer → **MilkDrop** (or `[visualizer.milkdrop]` in `config.toml`; see the [config reference](/reference/config/#milkdrop-mode)):

| Setting | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| Preset Interval | Seconds between automatic changes (0 to 600, default 30; 0 = never). |
| Preset Crossfade | Seconds one preset takes to dissolve into the next (0 to 10, default 2; 0 = a hard cut). Capped at half the Preset Interval. Two presets are drawn during a fade, so it costs more GPU while it lasts. |
| Change Preset With Track | A new track brings a new preset (default on). |
| Presets | `all`, `favorites_only`, or `nokkvi` (only Nokkvi's own presets). |
| Render Quality | Pixels rendered on the panel's shorter side before scaling: `low` (480), `medium` (720, default), `high` (1080), `native` (the panel's own size). Lower is softer and lighter on the GPU; `native` on a big window costs the most. |
| Show Preset Names | The name notification on each change (default on). |

## What it can't do (yet)

- **`.milk` files.** Only presets already converted to Butterchurn's JSON format load. Converting classic `.milk` presets needs a separate tool.
- **Presets that use images.** Presets that sample external textures are left out of the built-in pack; a user preset that needs one gets a generated stand-in (except `cover`, which is the playing album's).
- **Smooth transitions.** Presets cut from one to the next rather than blending. As in MilkDrop, each new preset starts from the previous one's picture rather than from black.
- **Animating while paused.** Pausing freezes the frame.

MilkDrop runs at up to 60 frames a second whatever your display's refresh rate, because presets are written for that speed.

<Aside type="caution" title="Before going back to an older Nokkvi">
  Nokkvi versions from before the MilkDrop mode can't read `visualization_mode = "milkdrop"`, and on start they reset **all** your settings to defaults. Switch the visualizer to another mode before you downgrade.
</Aside>
